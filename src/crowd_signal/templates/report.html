04 / Under the surface

Built to show its working.

01 — OBSERVE

Keep the evidence intact.

Social observations come through Apify; news comes through Exa. Duplicate and spam checks precede multilingual scoring. Long and short positions require explicit language. Missing engagement stays unknown.

02 — ALIGN

Respect the market clock.

Exchange calendars determine session boundaries. Features use information available by the prior close; after-close posts enter the following session. Newly retrieved history is never treated as known earlier.

03 — VALIDATE

Test forward through time.

Ridge and gradient boosting face random-walk and mean-return baselines. Five-session block bootstraps keep ticker groups together. Forecast intervals are empirical. Historical performance does not establish future accuracy.
