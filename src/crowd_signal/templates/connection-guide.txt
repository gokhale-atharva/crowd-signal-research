Crowd Signal — saved capture workflow

The public site displays saved reports. It does not stream data or run Python.
APIFY_TOKEN and EXA_API_KEY are configured in the ignored local .env. Never upload keys.
Both exchanges use LIHKG + Telegram. Exa supplies news for all ten companies.
Optional LLM_API_KEY enables model extraction; the current capture uses labelled lexicon scoring.

Review provider credits and source permissions before another capture. Each actor launch is
limited to20 items, a short timeout and at most$0.10 requested event charge. Compute/proxy
charges and Exa costs are separate. Do not relaunch an incomplete paid run blindly.

crowd-signal doctor --strict
crowd-signal run-all --exchange ALL --mode live
crowd-signal report

Already inspected Apify probes can be imported without another paid actor launch:
crowd-signal import-probes --exchange HKEX --selections data/raw/feasibility/HKEX_capture_inputs.json
crowd-signal analyse --exchange HKEX --mode live
crowd-signal train --exchange HKEX --mode live
Repeat for KRX. Partial acceptance requires explicit --allow-partial and remains labelled.
Re-publish generated reports to update the hosted site.

A company without relevant posts has no social score. A score of zero requires real observations.
Public channel samples include broker research and reposts; they are not a retail opinion census.
Recent posts are charted descriptively by publication time. Historical models only use evidence
that was observed before the relevant close. Current captures do not prove historical sentiment value.

Original price-only predictions target9November2026 and must remain immutable. Evaluate after
KRX12:00IST / HKEX13:30IST and only when genuine daily closing prices are available.
