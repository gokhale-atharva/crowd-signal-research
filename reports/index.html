03

Research notes

The social window is short. Any claim of predictive value needs a comparison against price alone.

Session alignment

Post timestamps normalize to UTC while preserving the original timezone. Posts at or after close map to the next observed session. The supplied market calendar accounts for weekends and holidays.

Sentiment and author positions

Direction describes sentiment. A long or short position is counted only when an author states it explicitly. Missing engagement remains unknown, rather than being recorded as zero. Analysis is not implemented at this checkpoint.

Forecast validation

Planned evaluation uses expanding windows, a random-walk baseline, and price-only / social / news ablations. Confidence intervals and paired comparisons must accompany results. No forecast or backtest has been produced yet.
